4. Methods
   Product of exponentials, space Jacobian, manipulability, damped least squares IK,
   recursive Newton-Euler, forward dynamics: RBE 501 (WPI) course notes and notation.
   The same formulation is published in K. M. Lynch and F. C. Park, Modern Robotics,
   Cambridge University Press, 2017.
   PD, PD with gravity compensation, feedback linearization (computed torque): RBE 502 (WPI).
   RRT-Connect: J. J. Kuffner and S. M. LaValle, "RRT-Connect: An efficient approach to
   single-query path planning," IEEE ICRA 2000.
   Quintic time scaling: RBE 501 lecture 19.
